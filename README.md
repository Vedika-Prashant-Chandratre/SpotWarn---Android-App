### 🚨 SpotWarn – Tourist & Citizen Safety Companion

**Developed by:** *Vedika Prashant Chandratre*
**Contributors:** *Anjali Pawar* & *Poonam Sapte*

**SpotWarn** is a mobile app designed to guide **tourists and citizens in Nashik** with **real-time safety information** about safe zones, crime-prone areas, parking, and emergency services. It also allows users to **report incidents**, while police can **monitor alerts** and take safety actions — especially during large events like **Kumbh Mela**.

📱 **Key Features:**

* Real-time map (works offline)
* Safe/unsafe zone detection (crime rate–based)
* SOS & “Shake to Safety” emergency alert
* Tourist spot, traffic, and parking guide
* Multilingual support for better accessibility

⚙️ **Tech Stack:**

* **Android Studio (Java, XML)**
* **Firebase (Backend, Authentication, Realtime DB)**
* **API Integration**
